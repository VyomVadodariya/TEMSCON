# AI Operations Control Center — Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)

## 1. Overview & Traceability Methodology

This Requirements Traceability Matrix maps every functional, experimental, security, and performance requirement of the **AI Operations Control Center (AIOCC)** under the **Authorized Research Design (Option A)** to its planned implementation location, verification method, and definitive pass/fail acceptance criteria.
